Shopify vs WooCommerce

🛍️ Shopify

Hosted · all-in-one · fastest to launch
  • Setup in days — hosting, security & updates handled
  • Beautiful themes + huge app ecosystem
  • Built-in payments, shipping & taxes
  • 24/7 support, scales with your growth
  • Best for: beginners & fast launches
VS

🔌 WooCommerce

Self-hosted · WordPress · total control
  • Free core plugin — pay only for hosting
  • Full ownership: data, design, code
  • Infinite customization via WordPress
  • No platform fees or revenue share
  • Best for: content-heavy & custom stores
